Beckley Health

A healthier, more beautiful world is possible.

Beckley Health is a public benefit corporation offering nature-based wellbeing and education programs, and working to widen access to them worldwide. Today that means legal psilocybin retreat programs in Jamaica and the Netherlands, shaped by ongoing research.

Our research

Access

Widening access is written into our charter. More than $300,000 in financial aid has gone to 67 guests, and the Amanda Feilding Memorial Fund supports those who would not otherwise have the means to attend.

Mission and the Memorial Fund

Standards

Every program includes a suitability call, preparation before and integration after, and experienced facilitators at a ratio of one to every four guests.

New places

We work only where our programs are fully legal. That is Jamaica and the Netherlands today, with more places in development.
