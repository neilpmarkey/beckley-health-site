Amanda Feilding

Our co-founder, and a defining figure in her field for five decades.

Amanda Feilding began asking questions about consciousness in her twenties and never stopped. In 1998 she founded the Beckley Foundation, named for Beckley Park, her family home in Oxfordshire, to bring rigorous science to substances the world had largely stopped studying.

Over the following decades her collaborations with institutions including Imperial College London and Johns Hopkins helped build the evidence base the field now stands on. She also worked directly with governments on policy, including in Jamaica.

Amanda co-founded Beckley Retreats in 2021. Her role was always one of vision: bringing scientific rigor, cultural respect and deep humanity together. She passed peacefully in May 2025, aged 82, surrounded by family and friends.

"I realized that it was only through science that one could overcome the taboo on these substances."

The Beckley Foundation

The research legacy we come from.

The Beckley Foundation was founded in 1998 to bridge the gap between scientific research and drug policy. It has funded, designed and co-authored research for over 25 years, including the Beckley/Imperial Psychedelic Research Programme with Imperial College London.

The Foundation is an independent research charity. Beckley Health shares its founding lineage and its commitment to evidence, and is a legally separate organisation.
